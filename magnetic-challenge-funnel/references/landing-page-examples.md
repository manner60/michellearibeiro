## Landing Page Best Practices

### Above the Fold (First Screen)
- Clear headline with big promise
- Subheadline supporting the promise
- Email capture form
- CTA button (contrasting color)

### Below the Fold
- Problem agitation
- Solution explanation
- Benefits (bullet points)
- Social proof
- FAQ (optional)
- Final CTA

### Design Tips
- Single column layout (mobile-first)
- Plenty of white space
- Large, readable fonts
- One goal: email capture
- Remove navigation distractions
- Fast loading speed

### Copy Tips
- Write at 8th-grade reading level
- Use "you" more than "I"
- Specific > vague (numbers help)
- Short paragraphs (2-3 lines max)
- Bullet points for scanning
- Urgency/scarcity near CTA
