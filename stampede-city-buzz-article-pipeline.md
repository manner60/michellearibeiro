# Stampede City Buzz - Article Pipeline

Below are 10 draft article ideas for review and future Article Cue use inside Letterman.

---

## 1. Why Small Community Events Are Becoming Calgary’s Real Social Scene
**Angle:** Big events still matter, but the city’s real energy is increasingly happening through smaller, neighborhood-level gatherings.
**Why it works:** Community-based, timely, and easy to link to local event culture.
**Use in newsletter:** Event / culture / city-life section.

---

## 2. Calgary Hidden Gems: The Local Places People Quietly Love
**Angle:** A roundup or feature piece about the kinds of local spots that become neighborhood favourites.
**Why it works:** Highly shareable and perfect for community identity.
**Use in newsletter:** Local Love / Hidden Gem section.

---

## 3. The Community Builders of Calgary: The People Who Bring Others Together
**Angle:** A tribute-style feature on organizers, volunteers, connectors, and local leaders.
**Why it works:** Strong human interest piece with recurring-series potential.
**Use in newsletter:** Community Spotlight.

---

## 4. Family-Friendly Calgary: Low-Key Things to Do Without Overplanning Your Weekend
**Angle:** Practical article for families looking for simple local activities.
**Why it works:** Useful, broad appeal, and easy to update weekly or seasonally.
**Use in newsletter:** What’s Happening / family-friendly content.

---

## 5. Why Supporting Local Feels More Personal in Calgary
**Angle:** Explore the emotional/community side of choosing local businesses over chains.
**Why it works:** Strong city-pride article that supports local culture without sounding overly commercial.
**Use in newsletter:** Good News / local love / community culture.

---

## 6. Spring in Calgary: Markets, Makers, and the Return of Local Energy
**Angle:** Seasonal article about spring markets, local makers, and renewed community activity.
**Why it works:** Timely and visually easy to support with images.
**Use in newsletter:** Seasonal roundup or feature article.

---

## 7. Calgary’s Creative Pulse: Why Local Arts Still Matter to Community Life
**Angle:** A city-culture piece on artists, venues, local performances, and the role of creativity in community identity.
**Why it works:** Gives the publication depth beyond business or events.
**Use in newsletter:** Arts & culture section.

---

## 8. How Neighbourhood Pride Shapes a Better City
**Angle:** Focus on how local identity, neighborhood support, and civic pride contribute to a stronger Calgary.
**Why it works:** Evergreen and aligned with the publication’s community-first mission.
**Use in newsletter:** Editorial / city-pride / opinion-lite feature.

---

## 9. Calgary Good News: The Stories Worth Paying More Attention To
**Angle:** Positive local developments, community initiatives, and quiet wins that often get overlooked.
**Why it works:** Great recurring format and highly aligned with feel-good community positioning.
**Use in newsletter:** Good News / City Pride section.

---

## 10. The New Calgary Social Life: Cafés, Pop-Ups, Walkable Events, and Community Spaces
**Angle:** Explore the shift toward smaller, more casual, more local social experiences.
**Why it works:** Modern, relatable, and rooted in how people actually spend time in the city.
**Use in newsletter:** Trend piece / events / lifestyle.

---

## Recommended First 4 Drafts to Create in Letterman

If we are creating article drafts first, I recommend starting with:

1. **Why Small Community Events Are Becoming Calgary’s Real Social Scene**
2. **The Community Builders of Calgary: The People Who Bring Others Together**
3. **Calgary Hidden Gems: The Local Places People Quietly Love**
4. **Calgary Good News: The Stories Worth Paying More Attention To**

These give you a strong foundation for recurring Article Cue content.
