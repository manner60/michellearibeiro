Welcome to the very first issue of **Stampede City Buzz** — a community-first roundup of what’s happening, who’s making a difference, and where Calgary is showing its best side.

## 🐝 THIS WEEK IN CALGARY

**Spring is finally starting to wake the city up.** Community markets are coming back, neighborhood patios are preparing to open, and event calendars are filling up again after a long winter stretch.

**Local momentum is shifting back to in-person connection.** Smaller community events, local vendor pop-ups, family activities, and grassroots gatherings are getting more attention than massive one-off experiences.

**Neighbourhood pride is on the rise.** More local groups are using social media and community boards to spotlight hidden gems, support small initiatives, and create tighter hyper-local networks.

**Calgarians are looking for simple things to do close to home.** Think markets, walks, cafés, local events, and affordable experiences that feel easy, social, and worth leaving the house for.

## 🌟 COMMUNITY SPOTLIGHT

**The people who build community rarely ask for attention — but they deserve it.**

Across Calgary, some of the most important city-building happens quietly. It happens through volunteers who organize local cleanups. Through market organizers who create space for makers. Through neighborhood connectors who know everyone by name and make new people feel welcome.

This issue’s spotlight is really for that whole category of people: the community builders.

The ones who start with a simple idea:
- a meetup
- a pop-up
- a local fundraiser
- a support group
- a street-level event
- or just a post asking neighbours to show up

Those efforts may seem small at first, but they’re often what make a city feel alive.

If you know someone in Calgary who is bringing people together, creating local energy, or making a neighborhood better, we want to hear about them.

## 📅 WHAT’S HAPPENING THIS WEEK

Here are a few types of events worth keeping an eye out for around the city this week:

### 🛍 Community Markets
Spring markets are beginning to return, bringing local makers, artists, food vendors, and small businesses together in one place. These are some of the easiest ways to support local and discover something new without planning your whole day around it.

### 🎨 Arts & Culture Events
Gallery shows, live performances, comedy nights, and community arts programming continue to give Calgary its creative pulse. Even one local event can completely change your week.

### 👨‍👩‍👧 Family-Friendly Outings
Weekend family events, library programming, recreation activities, and neighborhood gatherings are always worth watching — especially the lower-cost ones that make it easier for families to participate.

### ☕ Casual Meetups & Pop-Ups
Small pop-ups, vendor features, café events, and community-led hangouts are increasingly where some of the best city energy shows up first.

## 🙌 GOOD NEWS / CITY PRIDE

It’s easy to focus on what’s broken in a city.
It takes more intention to notice what’s working.

But Calgary gives us plenty to be proud of:
- neighbors showing up for each other
- volunteers giving their time
- local creators putting work into the community
- small businesses taking chances
- grassroots events bringing people out
- people choosing local over convenient

Cities are shaped by repetition.
And every time someone supports local, attends a community event, helps a neighbor, or shares something worth noticing, they help shape the version of Calgary we all get to live in.

That’s worth paying attention to.
