# Video Script Frameworks

## Welcome Video Script (2-3 minutes)

### Structure

**[0:00-0:15] HOOK**
"In the next [Duration] days, you're going to [Big Promise].

I'm [Your Name], and over [Timeframe], I've helped [Number] people go from [Before State] to [After State].

And I'm about to show you exactly how we do it."

**[0:15-0:45] WELCOME + EXPECTATIONS**
"Welcome to [Challenge Name]!

Here's how this works:
- Every day for [Duration] days, you'll get a short lesson
- Each lesson includes one simple task
- Tasks take 15 minutes or less
- Complete the task, share your progress
- Watch your transformation happen

**This only works if you participate.** Passive watching won't cut it."

**[0:45-1:30] WHAT'S COMING**
"Here's what we'll cover:
- Day 1: [Teaser]
- Day 2: [Teaser]
- Day 3: [Teaser]
...

By Day [Duration], you'll have [Specific Outcome]."

**[1:30-2:00] FIRST ACTION STEP**
"Your first task is simple:
[Specific action]

Do this before [Deadline].

Then head to our community and comment 'DONE' so I know you're in."

**[2:00-2:30] CLOSE**
"I'm excited to see your results.

See you in your inbox tomorrow at [Time].

[Your Name]"

## Daily Training Video Script (5-10 minutes)

### Structure

**[0:00-0:30] HOOK + RECAP**
"Welcome to Day [X]!

Yesterday we [recap previous day].

Today we're taking the next step: [Day's Objective].

This is important because [why it matters]."

**[0:30-2:00] TEACHING POINT**
"Here's what most people get wrong about [Topic]...

[Common misconception]

But the truth is: [Your insight/approach]

[Story or example illustrating the point]

[Key teaching content]

The key takeaway: [One sentence summary]"

**[2:00-3:30] ACTION STEP DEMONSTRATION**
"Here's your task for today:

[Task Name]

Let me show you exactly how to do it:

[Step 1]
[Step 2]
[Step 3]

[Screen share or visual demonstration if applicable]

Remember: This should take about [X] minutes. Don't overthink it."

**[3:30-4:30] ENGAGEMENT PROMPT**
"Once you've completed the task, here's what I want you to do:

[Specific engagement action — comment, post, share, etc.]

Why? Because [benefit of engagement — accountability, feedback, community].

Plus, I'll be personally reviewing every [post/comment] and giving feedback."

**[4:30-5:00] TEASE NEXT DAY + CLOSE**
"Tomorrow, we're covering [Teaser with open loop].

This is where it gets really interesting...

See you then!

[Your Name]"

## Pitch Video Script (10-15 minutes)

### Structure

**[0:00-1:00] HOOK + RECAP**
"Over the past [X] days, you've [recap transformation so far].

You've proven to yourself that this works.

But here's what you might be feeling right now...

[Acknowledge common emotion — excited but uncertain, want more, hitting a ceiling]

That's exactly where [Name] was [Timeframe] ago."

**[1:00-3:00] THE GAP**
"The challenge gave you the foundation. The proof of concept. The starting point.

But there's a gap between where you are now and [Ultimate Desired Outcome].

**The gap is:** [What's missing — time, advanced strategies, support, accountability, etc.]

Without bridging this gap, most people [negative outcome — plateau, give up, slow progress].

I don't want that for you."

**[3:00-5:00] THE SOLUTION**
"That's why I created [Offer Name].

[Offer Name] is [brief description].

It's specifically designed to [key benefit].

**Here's what you get:**
- [Component 1] — [Benefit]
- [Component 2] — [Benefit]
- [Component 3] — [Benefit]
- [Bonus 1] — [Benefit]
- [Bonus 2] — [Benefit]

Everything you need to [Ultimate Outcome]."

**[5:00-7:00] PROOF + CASE STUDIES**
"But don't just take my word for it.

**Meet [Name 1]:**
[Before state] → [After state] in [Timeframe]
"[Quote]"

**Meet [Name 2]:**
[Before state] → [After state] in [Timeframe]
"[Quote]"

**Meet [Name 3]:**
[Before state] → [After state] in [Timeframe]
"[Quote]"

These aren't outliers. These are typical results when you follow the system."

**[7:00-9:00] OFFER DETAILS**
"Here's exactly what you're getting:

[Detailed breakdown of offer components]

**Total value: $[X]**

**Your investment today: $[Y]**

**Plus these bonuses:**
- [Bonus 1] — Worth $[Value]
- [Bonus 2] — Worth $[Value]
- [Bonus 3] — Worth $[Value]

**Total bonus value: $[Z]**

**You get everything for just $[Y].**"

**[9:00-10:00] GUARANTEE**
"And here's my promise to you...

[Guarantee details — money back, results-based, etc.]

If [condition], just [action] and I'll [refund terms].

No questions. No hassle. No risk.

You have nothing to lose and [outcome] to gain."

**[10:00-11:00] URGENCY + SCARCITY**
"But there's a catch...

[Scarcity element — limited spots, time limit, etc.]

**Doors close [Deadline].**

After that, you'll have to wait until [Future Date] — and the price will be [Higher Price].

Don't let this opportunity pass."

**[11:00-12:00] CTA**
"Ready to [Ultimate Outcome]?

Click the link below and join [Offer Name] now:

[Link]

I'll see you inside!

[Your Name]"

## Quick Tips for Recording

1. **Energy matters** — Bring 10% more energy than feels natural
2. **Look at the lens** — Eye contact builds trust
3. **Use pauses** — Let key points land
4. **Show, don't just tell** — Demonstrate when possible
5. **End with energy** — Finish strong on the CTA
6. **Good enough > perfect** — Done is better than perfect
