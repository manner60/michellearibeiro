# PRODUCTION NOTES

## Equipment Needed:
- Screen recording software (OBS - free, or Loom)
- Decent microphone (built-in is fine to start)
- Good lighting (window light works)

## Editing Tips:
- Cut dead air and mistakes
- Add zoom-ins on important UI elements
- Use text overlays for key points
- Keep pacing brisk but not rushed

## Publishing Strategy:
1. Upload all 4 within 2 weeks
2. Use end screens to cross-promote
3. Pin comment with affiliate link
4. Respond to ALL comments in first 24 hours

## Success Metrics to Track:
- View duration (aim for 50%+)
- Click-through rate on links
- Comments asking for more
- Subscriber conversion
